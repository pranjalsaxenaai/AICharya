  Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension. Split by language.
- Tier-2 papers = subject-wise tests: English, Quant/Maths, Statistics
  (Paper-II, for JSO), GS/Finance & Economics (Paper-III / AAO).

  duplicate mirror copies (delete one set to slim down).
